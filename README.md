# Kingdom Chronicle Wiki

Independent English guide hub for **Kingdom Chronicle** at
`https://www.kingdomchronicle.wiki`.

The launch site covers:

- an evidence-backed beginner route for the opening turns;
- training, recruitment, diplomacy, alliances and reinforcements;
- all six troop types, officer skills, renown and mobilization;
- campaign victory conditions and all 18 Steam achievements;
- the July 23 Casual-difficulty update and official developer advice;
- official Windows requirements, review and safe Steam download;
- WebSite, Organization, VideoGame, VideoObject, FAQPage, ItemList, Article and
  BreadcrumbList structured data.

  Community are the primary identity and mechanics sources.

  extracted game files.
